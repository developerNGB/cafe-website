Welcome in

A room that suits every kind of day

Mornings are for espresso and pastries straight from the oven. Lunch slows down over soup, salads and sandwiches. Afternoons belong to anyone who needs a table, a plug socket and another coffee.

We keep the menu short so we can keep it good. Everything is made to order, the coffee is pulled to drink rather than to photograph, and there is always a pot of tea going.

How we work

Three things we keep simple

Coffee made to drink

Beans are ground to order and every shot is pulled when you ask for it, not before. Milk is steamed to order too.

A short, fresh kitchen

We cook a small menu each day so nothing sits around. What is on the board is what we made this morning.

Room to stay a while

Big tables, small tables, and a window bench. Come alone, come with the whole team, stay as long as you like.

Come and see us

There is always a chair free

Find us on Maple Street, five minutes from the station. Bring a book, bring a friend, or just bring ten minutes.
