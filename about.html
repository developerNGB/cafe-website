A small café that makes room for everyone

The Green Cup is a corner café with good light, a short menu and a habit of turning regulars into friends.

Our story

It started with one machine and a window seat

We wanted a place that worked at every hour of the day — quick enough for a coffee on the way to the station, slow enough for a two-hour lunch with an old friend.

So we kept the menu short, opened the windows, and let the room do the rest. The kitchen cooks what it would happily eat itself. The counter changes with the season. Nobody rushes you out.

Today it is still the same idea: turn up, take a seat, and stay for as long as the day allows.

What we believe in

Three promises we can keep

Nothing clever, just the things a neighbourhood café should get right every single day.

Quality

One coffee, done properly. Fresh milk, clean machines and staff who would rather remake it than hand you something poor.

Hospitality

You are greeted when you arrive and left alone when you want to be. Both matter, and we try to read which one you need.

Fresh, every day

Bread, pastry and prep arrive each morning. When something runs out it is gone until tomorrow, which is usually a good sign.

The room

Big windows, warm wood, and space to breathe

“We would rather be the place you come to three times a week than the place you visit once for a photograph.”

— The Green Cup team
